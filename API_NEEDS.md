NEED STATEMENTS

Team 9 needs  to  be able to read daily food budget in order to allow students to know how much money they can spend on food  daily.
Team 9 needs to read  delivery fee budget in order to allow students to know how much of their budget can be used for food delivery charges.
Team 9 needs to read remaining budget in order to show students how much money they have left after making food purchases and paying delivery fees.
Team 9 needs to view budget transactions in order to allow students to track how their food budget has been spent over time.
Team 9 needs to be able to update budget in order to allow students to change their allocated food and delivery budget when their spending limits change.
Team 9 needs budget deduction in order to automatically subtract the cost of food and delivery fees from the student's available budget after an order is made.

REFLECTION PARAGRAPH 
During our interview with Team 9, we were surprised that they needed more than just the student's total food budget. They also needed the remaining balance and spending history so students can make better decisions before placing orders. This helped us understand that our API needs to provide accurate and updated budget information.
